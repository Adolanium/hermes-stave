## Instrument samples

Playback downloads the FluidR3 GM soundfont, as converted by [midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), from `paulrosen.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts` when an instrument first plays. The samples are not part of this repository or the bundle.
